⚙️ How Email Sending Works

All emails go through Supabase — not the Omnis desktop app. When you compose an email, it is added to the queue in your Supabase database. A Supabase Edge Function runs every 5 minutes (via pg_cron) and sends any pending emails via SMTP. This means emails will be delivered even when Omnis is closed.
